# Capstone-Project 
## Description: Retail Sales & Operations Dashboard
This is my first project on Power BI

# Project Overview
This capstone project involved designing and developing a comprehensive Power BI dashboard to analyze and visualize key business metrics for a Portuguese e-commerce retail platform. The dataset, consisting of 9 interrelated tables, represents real-world commercial data that has been prepared for analytical use.

The primary goal was to turn raw data into actionable insights across areas like sales performance, order fulfillment, customer behavior, product feedback, and regional trends — all consolidated into a single, interactive dashboard.

## Project Objectives
The dashboard was built to answer eight specific business questions related to sales, logistics, product ratings, payments, and seasonal performance. The key objectives were:

1. Identify the top-selling product categories.
2. Analyze delayed order counts by product category.
3. Compare monthly delayed vs. on-time deliveries with detailed drill-through analysis.
4. Visualize customer payment preferences by method.
5. Display the top and bottom-rated products based on customer feedback.
6. Evaluate sales performance across different states in Portugal.
7. Detect seasonal sales trends every quarter.
8. Measure total revenue and observe how it evolves year-over-year.

## Steps Taken
**Data Import & Modeling:**
- All 9 datasets were imported into Power BI.
- Relationships were created using primary and foreign keys (e.g., customer ID, order ID, product ID).
- Fields were cleaned and standardized, and calculated columns were created to support analysis (e.g., “Is Delayed” flag).

**Data Calculation:**
- Created custom measures to calculate total revenue, order counts, average ratings, and delay statuses.
- Aggregated and segmented data to support category, state, monthly, and quarterly analysis.

**Visualization Development:**
- Used a combination of bar charts, pie charts, column charts, line graphs, and map visuals.
- Enabled interactive filtering and drillthrough to allow users to explore data from multiple perspectives.
- Included slicers for better user control and comparative analysis.

## Key Insights Delivered
- Identified top 10 categories contributing the most to total revenue.
- Highlighted which product categories had the highest number of delayed deliveries.
- Monthly trend analysis revealed periods with increased operational delays.
- Displayed a clear breakdown of customer payment method usage.
- Visualized the best and worst-rated products to guide inventory and quality decisions.
- Regional performance analysis showed strong and weak sales areas across different states.
- Quarterly sales patterns indicated distinct seasonal peaks.
- Year-over-year revenue tracking revealed growth trends and potential business cycles.
